app and its visible words read off the page (caps labels in their source case; the drawn status bar, the Test-flows panel and
screen-reader-only text left out; input placeholders as "(placeholder) …"). Lines with no words — glyphs, counts, wheel numbers, a bare
amount, an avatar's initials — are left out; amounts inside sentences are whatever the seed scenario prints (dollars only).

**How to read it.** Screens are grouped by persona and listed in the order of `docs/v5/SCREENS.md`. Under a screen, its first state prints
all its lines; every further state prints only the lines that screen has not shown yet ("no new lines" = the same words, a different
layout or data). Lines that appear on 8 or more screens are printed once, in section 1. **[COUNSEL]** marks a placeholder counsel
must write or approve; **[PLACEHOLDER]** marks a made-up value (Help's contact details) to replace before launch. Section 9 lists every
placeholder with where it lives.

**Not in this deck:** the gallery (`sys-components`), the screen index (`sys-index`) and the texts board (`sys-texts`, = section 7).
System-wide states (offline, session expired, the error toast, …) are Test-flows entries on a host screen; they print in the System
section with only what they add to that screen.

### `c-policy` — THE policy / legal page (D-380): garment protection (D-21, COUNSEL), cancellation and refunds, payments, disputes, safety + Terms, Privacy, Accessibility (S-23 / S-24, B2)

**`c-policy`** — Garment protection

- Garment protection
- Draft — counsel review required **[COUNSEL]**
- Draft of Oct 18, 2026
- What’s covered
- If a garment is lost or damaged while it’s with your tailor or the Taily courier, Taily pays to repair or replace it, up to $500 per order.
- That covers the fitting, the tailoring and the trip back to you.
- How to claim
- Tell us within 48 hours of getting your order back. Open the order and tap Get help, choose “A garment was damaged or lost”, and add photos.
- We compare the before and pinned photos from the fitting and the courier’s drop-off photo, then reply in the Help thread.
- A courier drop-off in your building’s lobby counts as delivered. If it isn’t there, tell us within 24 hours.
- Not covered
- Normal wear, a fabric failing with age, and changes you approved in the final order.
- What we pay
- We pay what it costs to repair the garment. If it can’t be repaired, we pay what it was reasonably worth when it was handed over. We may ask for a receipt or a photo of a similar item.
- The cap is $500 for all the garments in one order together. We pay back to the card used for the order, or by bank transfer.
- If the work itself isn’t what was agreed, that isn’t loss or damage. Tell us through Help and we work it out with you both.
- Who decides
- Taily decides each claim within 24 hours of having what we need, and tells you and your tailor in the Help thread. Taily Group LLC provides this protection through its insurer.

**`c-policy--cancellation`** — Cancellation and refunds

- Cancellation and refunds
- Before your tailor accepts
- If your tailor doesn’t accept within 2 hours, or declines, your deposit is released in full.
- Cancelling a visit
- Cancel for free until 24 hours before your visit. After that, your tailor keeps the deposit for the time set aside.
- 48 hours before your visit, we text you to confirm. Confirming doesn’t end your free window. If you don’t confirm by 3 hours before the visit, we cancel it and the deposit is kept.
- If your tailor cancels or doesn’t show up, your deposit is refunded in full.
- Refunds
- A refund goes back to the card you paid with. It can take 5 to 10 business days to show.
- The deposit
- When you send a request, we hold a deposit on the card: $10.00 of every $100.00 of the services and the home visit fee, shown in dollars before you send it. A hold is not a charge.
- We charge the deposit only when the tailor accepts. If the tailor declines, doesn’t answer within 2 hours, or the request is withdrawn first, the hold is released.
- The deposit counts toward the order. It is not an extra fee.
- The free change window
- A booked visit can be changed or cancelled for free until 24 hours before it starts. Confirming the visit doesn’t shorten that window.
- 48 hours before the visit we text the customer to confirm, and 26 hours before we remind her once more. If the visit is still not confirmed 3 hours before it starts, we cancel it and the tailor keeps the deposit. We only do this after a reminder reached her phone.
- Late cancellations and no-shows
- Inside the last 24 hours, a cancellation by the customer keeps the deposit for the tailor. If the customer isn’t there when the tailor arrives, that is a no-show and the tailor keeps the deposit. Nothing more is charged.
- If neither side records what happened, we ask you both. If nothing is recorded at all, we hold the deposit for 72 hours so either of you can tell us. With no answer, it goes to the tailor.
- When the tailor cancels
- If the tailor cancels, can’t make it, or doesn’t come, we refund the deposit in full, and the customer can book another tailor right away.
- The balance
- The final order is approved in the app at the fitting before any work starts. The balance is what is left after the deposit.
- When the tailor marks the order ready, we place a hold for the balance on the saved card. A hold is not a charge.
- The customer chooses pickup or delivery within 4 days. Every pickup time and delivery window we offer ends by the hold’s last day, 4 days after the order was ready. We charge the balance at the handoff: at pickup with the code, or when the courier delivers it.
- If the order isn’t handed over by the hold’s last day, we charge the balance then. If no way back was chosen by then, we also set it to in-store pickup and the tailor keeps the order until it’s collected; if the tailor has no shop, Taily contacts the customer to arrange the return.
- If the hold doesn’t go through, we ask the customer to update her card before the hold’s last day.
- A courier is priced again when the customer pays. If she later moves to a window that costs more, the difference is charged on its own.
- Questions about a charge
- Open the order and tap Get help. We reply within 24 hours. Taily Group LLC decides refund questions by what both sides recorded.
